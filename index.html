About Us

Your Wellness & Relaxation Destination in Patna

Welcome to Prana Spa Centre, a peaceful wellness and relaxation destination located in the heart of Sri Krishna Puri, Boring Road, Patna. Conveniently situated on Rajesh Kumar Path near GV Mall, we offer a calm and hygienic space designed to help you unwind from daily stress. Our experienced therapists provide personalized body massage, deep tissue therapy, aromatherapy, Swedish massage, and refreshing spa treatments tailored to your comfort. Whether you are looking for muscle pain relief, stress reduction, or complete rejuvenation, Prana Spa Centre delivers quality care in a soothing environment. Visit us near Verma Dental Esthetic in SK Puri, Patna, and book your relaxing wellness session today.
